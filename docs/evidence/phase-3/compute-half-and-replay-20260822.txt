So the changed argument is the driver's own rule giving the right answer on its first
outing, rather than an operator picking a different date.

----------

store whose ingest has not moved, reproduces what the backfill wrote byte for byte.

Method: snapshot every row for 2026-08-12 in the five compute tables; run all six
stages over that date through the NIGHTLY path, `Worker run <stage> 2026-08-12`; diff
the snapshot against the result with `EXCEPT` in both directions, whole rows, all

WHAT THIS REPLAY DOES NOT EVIDENCE, stated rather than left to be discovered:

  1. It is the compute half, not the literal evening order. The five ingest stages
     were skipped. A full `run-night` would have C02 reload twenty dates of bulk feed,
     giving 2026-08-13 to 2026-08-21 real bars; item 60's 5,729 stale rows on
     2026-08-13 would then stand on an ordinary ~3,000-bar session instead of being
     identifiable by their thin date. A verification step that conceals a known defect
     is worse than one that skips a stage, so it was not run.

  2. It does not exercise item 52's divergence class, and this date cannot. That class
     needs a ticker with a long price hole that is also a member on the replayed date.
     `OBNK.US`, the measured instance, has its last bar on 2026-02-05 and is not in the
     2026-08-12 population at all. No row on 2026-08-12 carries a null `rs_change_21d`.
     So this confirms the asserted half of the replay line and leaves the not-claimed
     half untested, which is what the amended line says it is for.

  The five `_replay_before_*` snapshot tables were dropped after the diff. The store
  already carries unexplained tables as an open item and this verification did not
  need to add five more; the diff result above is the evidence.

CORRECTION APPENDED AT THE SIGN-OFF REVIEW, 2026-08-22
-------------------------------------------------------
Appended rather than edited in place: this file is a captured transcript and the
figures above are what was derived at the time. Two of them were derived wrongly and
the corrections are here so a reader meets them beside the originals.

  "over 1,462 of 1,462 dates" is 1,457. indicator_daily and sentiment_derived_daily
  both hold 1,457 distinct in-window dates and both begin 2021-01-11. The window's
  first five sessions, 2021-01-04 to 2021-01-08, carry ~3,700 price bars each and no
  computed row, because security_daily's earliest row is 2021-01-10 and a
  membership-keyed stage has no epoch to resolve before it. Every date the table does
  hold is covered, so the percentage above stands and the date coverage does not.

  "1,641,317 of the 1,644,015 rows that carry a value at all, 99.8 percent" puts an
  in-window numerator over a whole-table denominator. In-window rows carrying
  article_count_z_own_90d are 1,642,154, giving 99.95 percent. 1,644,015 is correct
  over the whole table of 3,814,766.

Both re-measured against the developer store at the review. PROGRESS.md carries the
corrections with the mechanism.
